Aesthetic Dermatology

Explore our laser, skin rejuvenation and injectable treatments. Every treatment starts with an individual assessment at Zahra Health Clinic in Bahria Town, Karachi.

Book a Consultation

Treatments & Services

Laser Hair Removal (Soprano Titanium)

Smooth skin without constant shaving or waxing. Our double-cooling laser keeps sessions comfortable, and settings are chosen for darker skin tones.

Ideal for: Face, underarms, arms, legs and bikini line. Plan: A course of sessions. Downtime: Usually none; mild redness for a few hours.

Book Treatment

CO2 Laser Resurfacing

Fractional CO2 laser removes damaged layers and triggers fresh collagen, helping acne scars, rough texture, large pores, fine lines and early skin laxity.

Ideal for: Acne scars, uneven texture, enlarged pores and mild sagging. Downtime: Redness and peeling for several days; sometimes longer.

Book Treatment

Acne & Scars Treatment

We treat active acne first, then plan the right scar approach - laser, microneedling, peels or a combination - around your scar type.

Ideal for: Active acne, boxcar, rolling and pitted scars. Plan: Combination plan over several visits.

Book Consultation

HydraFacial

Deep cleansing, extraction and hydration for fresh, glowing skin. Suitable for tired, dull, uneven or dehydrated skin.

Book Treatment

Microneedling & PRP Face

Microneedling stimulates collagen to smooth texture and scars. PRP made from your own blood supports repair and brings back glow.

Downtime: Mild redness or swelling, usually short.

Book Treatment

Skin Boosters: PDRN & Polynucleotide

Injectable boosters for deep hydration, elasticity and better skin quality, with a soft, healthy finish.

Downtime: Small bumps settle within a day or two.

Book Treatment

Chemical Peels

Medical-grade peels for pigmentation, acne marks and rough texture, chosen by strength to suit your skin.

Plan: Repeated at intervals. Downtime: Light flaking for a few days.

Book Treatment

Exosomes

A regenerative therapy that supports healing and glow, often paired with microneedling.

Ideal for: Post-procedure recovery, dull or damaged skin. Downtime: Minimal.

Book Treatment

Hands & Feet Rejuvenation

Improve texture, volume and tone so your hands and feet look as cared for as your face.

Ideal for: Thin, crepey or pigmented skin. Downtime: Usually minimal.

Book Treatment

Botox

Softens frown lines, forehead lines and crow's feet while keeping your expression natural. Results typically last three to four months.

Book Treatment

Dermal Fillers

Restore lost volume, define cheeks, chin and lips, and smooth deeper lines with carefully placed filler. Results often last six to twelve months.

Book Treatment

Thread Lifting

A non-surgical lift for mild to moderate sagging along the jawline, cheeks and neck.

Downtime: Some swelling or bruising for several days.

Sculptra & Radiesse Biostimulators

Support your skin's natural collagen rebuilding so firmness and contour improve gradually and look natural.

Ideal for: Early to moderate laxity and lost firmness. Downtime: Minimal.
