Frequently Asked Questions

Why should I choose a skin clinic in Karachi?
Choosing a skin clinic should be based on professional qualifications, treatment experience, appropriate technology, consultation quality, and whether the clinic provides treatment suited to your individual needs.

How do I know which aesthetic treatment is right for me?
The appropriate treatment depends on your skin condition, concern, treatment history and goals. A consultation allows the doctor to assess your concern and discuss suitable options.

Do you provide laser treatments for different skin types?
Laser treatment suitability depends on the specific technology, skin type, hair characteristics, treatment area and individual circumstances. A professional assessment should be completed before treatment.

Do I need a consultation before an aesthetic treatment?
A consultation is recommended because not every treatment is appropriate for every patient. It also gives you an opportunity to discuss expected results, possible risks, recovery and aftercare.

Do aesthetic treatments provide permanent results?
Results vary depending on the treatment and individual factors. Some treatments require maintenance or multiple sessions. During your consultation, you can discuss realistic expectations for your selected treatment.
