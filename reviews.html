Treatments Our Patients Review Most

People visit our laser and skin clinic in Karachi for many reasons. These are the treatments patients talk about most, and what to expect from each.

Laser skin resurfacing & CO2 laser

Patients with acne scars, uneven texture, enlarged pores or early signs of ageing often ask about CO2 laser resurfacing. Results build over weeks rather than overnight, and Dr. Hina assesses your skin first.

Laser facial & laser treatment for face

A laser facial can help with dullness, pigmentation, scarring and texture. Your plan is built around your skin type and goals, not a fixed package.

Laser hair removal for dark skin

Darker skin needs suitable technology and careful settings. Suprano Titanium uses double cooling, and your doctor chooses settings for your skin tone and hair type.

Skin tightening & lifting

Thread lifts, skin boosters, biostimulators and CO2 laser may be considered according to your age, skin laxity and preferred downtime.

Medical dermatology & diabetes care

Our specialists treat acne, melasma, hair fall and nail conditions, while Dr. Ahmed Khalil provides diabetes care.
